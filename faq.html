Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about Corten steel, house numbers, mounting and NZ shipping from our Gisborne workshop.

What is Corten steel — will it stain walls or timber?

Corten (weathering steel) forms a stable, protective rust-like patina outdoors instead of rusting away like ordinary mild steel. That’s the warm orange-brown look people love.

While it weathers, it can leave run-off stains on light walls, pale timber, concrete or paving below. For clean faces or light cladding, mount with a small stand-off, use a drip edge, or place numbers where runoff won’t mark anything important. On dark fence timber the effect is usually minor once the surface has stabilised.

Raw vs pre-weathered — what do I get?

Most pieces leave our workshop as raw 3 mm Corten — a mill finish that will weather naturally once outdoors (speed depends on rain, salt air and sun).

If you need a more developed look from day one, ask about pre-weathering options when you order. Either way, the steel keeps changing slightly over the first months until the patina settles.

House number heights (100–300 mm) — how big do they look?

Height is the character height (top to bottom of a digit or letter), not including mounting gap:

  • 100 mm — compact; letterbox / close-up doors
  • 150–200 mm — popular for most houses and fences
  • 250–300 mm — bold for long driveways and rural gates (readable from further away)

Use the live preview on the House Numbers page, or message us a photo of your post/fence and we’ll suggest a size.

Clean face vs pre-drilled mounting?

Clean face — no fixing holes in the character. Ideal if you want a seamless look and will use adhesive, stand-offs, or your own mounting method.

Pre-drilled — mounting holes cut in each character so you can screw straight to timber, posts or cladding. Faster DIY install; the holes are part of the finished look (as on many of our product photos).

How long does it take? Are you made in Gisborne?

Yes — every piece is profile-cut in Gisborne, New Zealand from 3 mm Corten steel.

Lead times depend on the queue and order complexity. Simple house number sets are usually turned around promptly; custom DXF jobs may take longer. We’ll confirm timing when you order or request a quote. Need it for a specific date? Tell us — we’ll be honest about what’s realistic.

NZ shipping — standard vs rural cost?

We ship NZ-wide. At checkout, shipping is estimated from parcel weight/size.

Standard — town, city and normal street delivery.

Rural — RD / rural delivery addresses cost more (an extra rural surcharge on top of the base rate), because couriers charge more for those runs. Type your address in the cart — if it looks rural we’ll flag it, or you can choose Rural yourself.

Can I order custom shapes / DXF?

Yes. Upload a DXF (or describe what you need) on the Custom Quote page. We profile-cut custom signage, garden pieces and one-offs from 3 mm Corten.

Clean closed outlines work best. If you’re unsure about the file, send it through anyway — we’ll check and reply with a price.

Care outdoors — do I need to maintain it?

For most outdoor installs: leave it alone. Rain and air develop the protective patina. You don’t paint or oil it like timber.

Avoid trapping constant wet mud against the steel or sealing it under a finish that stops it “breathing.” A rinse with a hose if it gets filthy is fine. Over time the colour deepens and settles — that’s normal, not a fault.
